Tanjiro Kamado (竈門 炭治郎)

Sun Breathing Prodigy • Demon Hunter • Voice: Natsuki Hanae (JP) / Zach Aguilar (EN)

Stream Series

Origin Lore & Dossier

Tanjiro Kamado is a noble warrior who lived peacefully in Mount Kumotori until his family was slaughtered by Muzan Kibutsuji, progenitor of all demons. After discovering his surviving sister Nezuko transformed into a demon, Tanjiro pledged his life to the Demon Slayer Corps to find a cure and avenge his kin.

Blessed with an extraordinary sense of smell capable of detecting the "opening thread" of lethal opportunity in mortal combat, Tanjiro trained under former Water Hashira Sakonji Urokodaki on Mount Sagiri. In extreme life-or-death duels, he awakened Hinokami Kagura, the mythical Sun Breathing inherited through his family's Kagura ritual.

Signature Breathing Forms & Moves

Hinokami Kagura • Dance (Enbu) Ultimate Solar

A singular, high-velocity slashing strike charged with blinding solar thermal energy capable of severing Upper Rank demonic regenerative cells.

Water Breathing • Tenth Form: Constant Flux Fluid Attack

A continuous rotating slash that gains kinetic momentum with each rotation, manifesting a giant flowing blue water dragon.

Transparent World • Selfless Domain Sensory State

Allows Tanjiro to perceive the muscle contractions, blood flow, and respiratory shifts of opponents in slow motion with zero fighting spirit.

Climax Battles & Chronicles

Swordsmith Village Battle vs Hantengu (Upper Four)

Tanjiro wields Yoriichi Type Zero's ancient black Nichirin blade and unleashes Dragon Sun Halo Dance to incinerate Hantengu's clones at dawn.

Mugen Train Encounter vs Akaza (Upper Three)

Fought alongside Flame Hashira Kyojuro Rengoku, defending 200 sleeping passengers against Enmu before witnessing the catastrophic arrival of Akaza.
